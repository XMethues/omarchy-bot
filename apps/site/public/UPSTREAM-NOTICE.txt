Credit belongs to the Omarchy website contributors. The official redesign

https://omarchy.org/news/2026/09/omarchy-org-redesign-launches-with-29-languages/

Omarchy Bot adapts the renderer to a native TypeScript lifecycle, adds explicit
motion controls, and does not include the radio or etch-effect integrations.
The hero wordmark adds BOT lettering to the original Omarchy artwork.
